======================================================
WORD SEARCH PUZZLE GENERATOR - SUPPORT & DOCUMENTATION
======================================================

[Game Overview]
A procedural 15x15 terminal-based word search. Words are hidden across 
8 distinct directions (Horizontal, Vertical, and Diagonals). Words can 
interlock and share characters.

[How to Play]
1. Launch the executable.
2. Enter your Player Name to initialize your Player Profile.
3. Choose your word bank (Load from words.txt OR type your own).
4. Select the number of words to hide.
5. Use the Action Menu to navigate the game loop.

[Controls & Actions]
[1] Guess a word : Type a word you found. If correct, it highlights green.
[2] Reveal       : Temporarily reveals all hidden words in yellow.
[3] Hint         : Provides the starting row, column, and direction of a word.
[4] Regenerate   : Keeps your current words but generates a completely new grid.
[5] New puzzle   : Restarts the setup phase for new words.
[0] Quit         : Ends the session and prints your total score profile.

[Troubleshooting]
- Missing File Error: If 'words.txt' is deleted, the C engine will automatically 
  re-create it with a default tech-word dataset upon launch.
- Formatting Issues: If the grid looks misaligned, ensure your terminal font 
  is set to a Monospace font (like Consolas or Courier New).
- IDE Terminals: For the best experience, run the compiled program in a native 
  terminal (Windows Terminal, CMD, bash) rather than an IDE's integrated output 
  tab to allow screen clearing (system("cls")) to function smoothly.
